        About this documentary

        What you are seeing

        Every 3D scene in this prototype is an artistic reconstruction generated in code. None of it is NASA photography or footage. The rovers, landers, rocket and airbags are stylized models, not engineering-accurate NASA models. Mars and Moon globes are procedurally painted maps that place major real features (maria, Syrtis Major, Hellas, Tharsis, polar caps and others) at their approximate real coordinates. The Earth texture comes from the three.js examples repository and is based on NASA imagery.

        All narration is original documentary writing. It is not an authentic NASA transmission or recording. The optional voice is your browser’s speech synthesis.

        Coordinates

        Site coordinates follow published NASA landing-site positions (planetocentric, east longitude). Opportunity and Spirit markers show landing sites; their final positions are nearby at globe scale. The Ingenuity marker uses the Perseverance landing site in Jezero crater and is approximate. The Apollo 17 marker shows the Lunar Module site; the rover was parked a short distance away.

        Technology

        Built with three.js, the Web Audio API and the Web Speech API. Typefaces: Cormorant Garamond, Inter, IBM Plex Mono (Google Fonts).
